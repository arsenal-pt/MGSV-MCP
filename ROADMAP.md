# Roadmap

1. Improve independent review and synthetic regression coverage of AI-written source and boundaries.
2. Expand accurate primary-source references and robust targeted refresh/error reporting.
3. Add authored format fixtures and byte/field-level round-trip checks for more converter variants.
4. Improve adapter option/version/runtime detection while keeping GUI/reference support distinct.
5. Measure indexing/job costs and improve portability, packaging and reproducibility.
6. Broaden independently verified native format/editor workflows. Game deployment and gameplay acceptance remain separate tasks.

The purpose is dependable research and staged conversion, not a claim of fully automatic asset extraction, native room creation or complete engine coverage. Maintain source/credit/license provenance for additions. Active development and contributions are welcome.
