Historical indexed revision: `2326cdf753a6fcfefb1867dd66ed97a078d4d114`; fresh syncs can differ. Wiki text/assets are fetched into ignored local data, not copied into this source release.

- Atvaark, BobDoleOwndU, kapuragu and other converter contributors. Pinned download origins and hashes are in tool-releases.json. No converter implementation/binary is vendored here.
- FoxKit/FoxLib authors (including Joey35233 and youarebritish), FMDL Studio's BobDoleOwndU and contributors, SnakeBite/MakeBite's TinManTex and contributors, Infinite Heaven/IHHook authors, SideOp Companion/File Monolith authors, Blender and other editor/framework/research contributors linked in the catalog.
- Ninja Ripper and bundled importer authors: optional legacy package provenance is linked in scripts/install_ninja_ripper.py; separate upstream terms apply. No ripper/importer binary or bundled help is redistributed.
- Konami and the original developers/artists for Metal Gear Solid V and FOX Engine. Game files and trademarks remain with their rights holders. This is an unofficial, unaffiliated project.
