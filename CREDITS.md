# Credits and acknowledgements

**The MGSV modding community made this workbench possible.** Thank you to the modders, reverse engineers, tool authors, document writers, testers and maintainers whose work it connects. Their projects retain their own authorship; linking, indexing or adding an adapter does not make their work ours.

This page gives direct project and person credits. The [complete 211-entry attribution index](docs/TOOL-AUTHORS.md) also covers discovery repositories, utilities, guides and references; its [structured source records](docs/tool-attribution.json) preserve the basis for each attribution. An entry in the catalog does not mean its code was incorporated or that we used/executed it. Repository ownership is labeled separately when original authorship has not been established.
